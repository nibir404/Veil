# VEIL — The Ten Missions

Each mission is a dense, handcrafted sandbox (roughly 0.2–0.6 km², with vertical and interior layers) centred on one division. Each has its own visual identity, rhythm, tone, hazards, AI flavour, traversal, and psychological theme. Every mission supports the six routes (**Rooftop · Ground · Social · Underground · Water · Direct**) where the geography allows, and every assassination supports multiple approaches.

Legend: **⟐ Intel** (feeds the board) · **✕ Surreal beat** · **◆ Set piece** · **☍ Carry-over** (changes a later mission)

---

## 01 — THE CITY THAT NEVER SLEEPS
**Dhaka · Paranoia · Monsoon night** — *playable in this slice*

**Space:** A block of Old Dhaka under an elevated MRT line. Main road with rickshaw, CNG and bus traffic (left-hand); brick-paved bazaar lane under blue and orange tarps; 4 m back lanes; 3–7-storey buildings with grills, *chhajja*, balconies, AC units; roofs of black water tanks, rebar stubs, clotheslines, *chilekotha* stair rooms; tangled cables on every pole; pole-top transformers. The full production adds Shankhari-Bazar-style lanes, a modern Gulshan glass tower, a metro station interior, an under-construction site, and underground drainage passages.

**Target:** THE CARTOGRAPHER — an intelligence broker meeting on the roof of *Rahman Telecom*, guarded by Nirapotta Solutions PMC. **He seems to know Raven is coming**: he turns and looks at Raven's position through walls.

**Routes:** Rooftop (climb any facade, gap-jump the row) · Ground (back lane; booth; front door) · Social (steal a Nirapotta jacket off a rooftop line) · Underground (full build: drain culvert from the bazaar into the building's generator room) · Direct (take a KR-7 from a guard).

**Approaches in the slice:** long range from the opposite roof · silent takedown · social (disguise) · **environmental** (shoot the leaking diesel generator next to the meeting) · tactical assault · information-driven (radio log reveals the leak, CCTV reveals the escape route).

**⟐ Intel:** phone SMS (location) · Nirapotta radio log (patrols + tap their channel) · CCTV terminal · envelope: `TARGET: RAHMAN / STATUS: KILLED / OPERATIVE: RAVEN`.

**◆ Set piece:** the target flees across four rooftops in monsoon rain, down a stairwell, to a car on the east road. Stop him.

**✕ Surreal:** man in the white panjabi (vanishes when you look away; "What man?") · dry-street alley (rain stops, city silent, then resumes — "Is it still raining where you are?") · CCTV three minutes in the future · the radio speaks to Raven on the way out.

**Ending:** The identification photograph in the Cartographer's jacket shows **Raven**, dated 12.03.2019. **Who was hunting whom?** Debrief: *"We'll talk about the photograph tomorrow." / "…I never told you about the photograph."*

**☍** Detection count sets how many Nirapotta units recognise Raven's silhouette in Mission 10.

## 02 — THE PORT
**Chattogram · Conspiracy · Coastal night, sea fog rolling in**

**Space:** A container terminal with gantry cranes (STS and RTG), straddle carriers, reefer stacks humming, container canyons; a customs warehouse; coastal road up to a hill (Batali-Hill-style) with an overlook; the edge of a shipbreaking beach (Sitakunda-style) where beached hulls are cut open under floodlights.

**Target/objective:** Intercept a weapons shipment in container `MSKU 7741…` and eliminate the fixer, **Haroon Siddiqui**, who runs night shifts in the crane cabin.

**Routes:** Rooftop = container tops & crane booms (crane cable traversal) · Ground = container canyons, forklift lanes · Social = dock-worker overalls with a hard hat; ID badge scanner check-points · Underground = storm drains under the stack yard · Water = swim from the channel, climb anchor chains of a moored feeder ship · Direct.

**Approaches:** drop a container on him from the gantry (environmental) · crane-cabin takedown 50 m up · sniper from the hill · sabotage the reefer power to black out a stack · info-driven: his shift rota lets you wait in his car.

**AI flavour:** customs officers vs. private security vs. dockworkers (who are civilians, not enemies — hurting them has consequences). Guards use forklift headlights as searchlights. Fog pushes AI to closer, paired patrols.

**Hazards:** moving straddle carriers, swinging loads, reefer electrics, slick steel in rain, tidal water level.

**◆ Set piece:** stealth infiltration escalates into a **firefight inside the container stacks** with doors opening as cover, cranes moving overhead and a straddle carrier becoming a moving shield.

**Reveal:** The container holds no weapons. It holds racks of **biometric identity records** — thousands. Retina maps, fingerprints, gait profiles. One drawer: **RAVEN**, with records spanning 11 years.

**✕ Surreal:** a crane operator waves at Raven by name from the cabin; later the cabin is empty and dusty. Container numbers repeat in impossible order.

**⟐** Biometric index · shipping manifest listing "JANUS / KHULNA" · a gait-analysis printout of *Raven walking* in Mission 01.

**☍** Taking the records (heavy, slows you) vs. destroying them changes what the Mission 07 archive contains.

## 03 — THE GHOST FARM
**Rajshahi · Identity · Golden late afternoon → dusk**

**Space:** Mango orchards in rows; wheat and mustard fields; mud-brick village with tin roofs, cow sheds, tube-wells; a raised district road with a slow market; the Padma's dry-season char (sandbanks); an abandoned agricultural research station with fenced greenhouses and a derelict lab block.

**Objective:** Find **Dr. Nasreen Akhter**, a PROJECT JANUS scientist missing for three years.

**Rhythm:** slow, sunlit, *wrong in daylight*. The first long stretch has no enemies at all.

**Social stealth:** villagers greet Raven warmly: *"Apni abar ashchen?" — You've come back again?* Several insist he visited "last winter" and "stayed at Kalam bhai's." Raven insists he has never been here. The player may choose dialogue that plays along or denies — both are logged.

**Routes:** Ground (orchard rows as cover lanes — tree canopy blocks drones) · Social (help at a mango market stall to pass the checkpoint) · Water (irrigation canal) · Rooftop (research-station roofs and water tower) · Direct.

**AI flavour:** a private "agricultural security" unit patrols with a drone; heat haze degrades both their optics and Raven's scope; dogs in the village react to Raven.

**◆ Set piece:** **sniper sequence overlooking farmland** from the research station water tower at golden hour — wind, heat shimmer, bullet drop over 400 m; the target convoy moves along the raised road between mango trees.

**Reveal:** inside an abandoned house, a wall of **photographs of Raven taken years before his supposed recruitment** — at a village wedding, fishing on the Padma, asleep on a bench — some with Dr. Akhter.

**✕ Surreal:** the same child brings Raven tea in three separate houses. A photograph on the wall is different when Raven looks back at it.

**⟐** Dr. Akhter's field journal ("subject R3 retains procedural memory, episodic replacement stable at 71%") · a village ledger with Raven's handwriting.

**☍** If the player accepted the villagers' story, Dr. Akhter's voice notes in Mission 07 address Raven by a different name.

## 04 — THE DEAD WATER
**Khulna / Sundarbans · Isolation · Night fog on the mangroves**

**Space:** A long river approach from an industrial Khulna jetty (jute mills, a disused power plant) into narrowing Sundarbans channels; mangrove roots, mudflats at low tide, a forest-department watchtower, a half-submerged honey-collectors' camp, an abandoned fish-processing station on stilts.

**Objective:** Track and eliminate a rogue operative, codename **HERON**.

**Rhythm:** long stretches of near-silence. Water, insects, wind, distant engines, the hull knocking, Raven's breathing. The player pilots a small wooden boat (engine = noise; paddle = silence; drifting with tide = free but uncontrolled).

**Routes:** Water (boat, swim, dive under jetties, underwater hiding among roots) · Ground (mudflats — slow, loud footsteps, sinking) · Rooftop (stilted station roofs, watchtower) · Direct.

**AI flavour:** river-pirate crew hired as sentries; they fire flares that light the fog in a red dome; they hunt by boat with spotlights; they fear the forest at night and won't enter narrow channels — which becomes a route.

**Hazards:** tide changes the map (channels close, mudflats open), crocodile-inhabited stretches (avoid, never fight — natural world is not a target), fog, darkness.

**◆ Set piece:** **boat infiltration through the Sundarbans at night** — cut the engine, drift under the pirate lookout's spotlight, slip into the water, board their boat from below.

**✕ Surreal (core):** Raven's radio begins transmitting **his own voice**: *"Don't trust the mission."* Later: *"You've already killed him."* The player cannot establish whether the transmission is real (the waveform appears on the handset; Zahir hears nothing).

**Reveal:** Heron's camp contains Raven's gear — with Raven's blood type, Raven's knife, a mission report for this exact operation *filed last December*.

**☍** Whether you kill Heron or let him go determines who is in the second chair in Mission 10's staging (the ending remains ambiguous either way).

## 05 — THE HOUSE OF RAIN
**Sylhet · Memory · Heavy rain in the tea hills, mist**

**Space:** Terraced tea gardens with shade trees; labourers' lines; a colonial-era tea estate bungalow with verandas, teak interiors, a gramophone, portraits; a narrow hill road with a small bazaar; a waterfall stream; mist that erases the top of every hill.

**Target:** **Colonel (retd.) Mahbub Chowdhury**, former intelligence officer.

**Routes:** Ground (tea rows — crouch height, rain-masked) · Social (estate labourer; carrying a tea basket) · Rooftop (bungalow tin roofs, deafening in rain — sprinting is loud) · Underground (cellar and old drainage from the estate's factory) · Direct.

**AI flavour:** estate guards plus the colonel's old bodyguards (veterans: they check behind doors, don't fall for thrown noises twice).

**◆ Set piece:** **close-quarters combat inside the tea estate mansion** — shotgun and knife range, lamps knocked over, rain pouring through a broken skylight, a gramophone stuck on a loop.

**The turn:** the colonel does not attack. He puts his hands on the table. *"You're not the assassin."* He shows classified footage: **Raven conducting Missions 01–04**. The footage is dated **months earlier**. The player can kill him, spare him, or walk out mid-sentence.

**✕ Surreal:** every clock in the house shows a different time; one room is dry despite the broken roof; the colonel calls Raven "beta" (son).

**⟐** The footage (plays on the board) · a file: `TARGET: RAHMAN / STATUS: ACTIVE`.

**☍** Sparing the colonel adds his voice to the Mission 10 radio chatter.

## 06 — THE EMPTY VILLAGE
**Rangpur · Absence · Dawn fog, then flat white overcast**

**Space:** Flat fields to the horizon, a village of tin-and-bamboo homes, a primary school, a tea stall, a rice mill, a bamboo bridge over a canal, a transformer on a pole humming, a single paved road.

**Premise:** nobody lives here — but **fans are running, food is warm, televisions are on, radios play, lights are active, footprints in the mud are fresh.**

**Rhythm:** exploration-horror with no conventional enemies for the first third. Tension comes from absence and the sense of being watched (a CCTV dome on every home, some tracking Raven).

**Gameplay:** investigation layer peaks: timestamps on the food, the TV schedule, the rice-mill log show the village "resets" every 40 minutes. The player learns the loop and uses it.

**Enemies:** later, a clean-up unit in unmarked uniforms arrives to "reset" the village — they behave like stagehands, not soldiers. They carry clipboards and suppressed weapons.

**Reveal:** the village was a **psychological conditioning facility**. Under the school: observation rooms, one-way glass, subject files. Subject R-3's file has Raven's childhood drawing in it.

**✕ Surreal:** a TV shows Raven walking into the house he's standing in; a radio plays the Mission 01 tea-stall news broadcast; the same meal is on every table.

**☍** Choosing to "stay" for one full loop (optional) unlocks a false memory that appears as a Veil fragment for the rest of the game.

## 07 — THE MEMORY ROOM
**Mymensingh · Conditioning · River light, then underground fluorescent**

**Space:** The old Brahmaputra riverbank, an institutional campus (colonial brick, overgrown), a disused hospital wing, a flooded basement; below it, an **archive of hundreds of rooms**, each a reconstructed room from a person's life (a Dhaka bedroom, a launch cabin, a classroom), filed by subject number.

**Objective:** find the archive's index and your own room.

**Gameplay:** the archive is a navigable memory maze with locked-in logic: rooms can be entered, but their doors lead to other subjects' rooms depending on what you touched. Security are **archivists** — non-combat custodians who raise alarms — and a hunter unit.

**◆ Set piece:** **the underground memory archive** — a sustained stealth sequence through recreated rooms, where cover is furniture from strangers' childhoods.

**Reveal:** Raven's room. **It does not match the life he remembers**: a different mother's sari on the chair, a different name on school certificates, a cricket bat he has never owned — but his handwriting in the diary.

**✕ Surreal:** the board begins to contain cards the player never collected, in Raven's handwriting. Integrity drops below 50%.

This is where the player should seriously question whether Raven's identity is authentic.

## 08 — THE LAST BOAT
**Barishal · Escape · Night, dense river fog, lantern light**

**Space:** A launch terminal with multi-deck passenger launches (painted hulls, cabins, crowded decks, tea sellers), a floating market of wooden boats, river villages on stilts, a remote char island, docks and pontoons.

**Objective:** an extraction order — leave Bangladesh on a launch bound for the Bay.

**Rhythm:** begins social (buy a ticket, find a cabin, blend with passengers), becomes a mystery (**passengers disappear**), escalates into a boat-to-boat escape.

**Reveal:** the launch is not transporting people. **It is transporting identities** — the cargo hold is an encrypted records vault; people are assigned new names, histories and identities. Raven finds his own "assignment" — and evidence his identity was **created in the same system**. `RAHMAN NEVER EXISTED`.

**◆ Set piece:** **escape through the Barishal waterways** — jump between launches and country boats in fog, fight on a moving deck, dive under a pontoon, steal a speedboat through the floating market.

**✕ Surreal:** the passenger manifest lists Raven in three cabins; the tea seller from Mission 01's stall is aboard and doesn't recognise him.

## 09 — THE DUPLICATE
**Cumilla · Duality · Hard midday sun → storm**

**Space:** Dense town neighbourhoods at the edge of farmland; a military checkpoint near border infrastructure; Buddhist-vihara-style archaeological ruins (Mainamati-like terraces of brick); an abandoned military compound; tunnels under the ruins into a JANUS relay facility.

**Encounter:** another operative. **Same face, same voice, same scars, same memories.** He says: *"You're the copy."*

**Gameplay:** a mirrored stealth-hunt — the Duplicate uses the player's own tactics (he has learned from the player's rating data across Missions 01–08: if you favoured rooftops, he watches roofs). Then a multi-phase fight across the ruins and into the tunnels: gunplay, a knife duel, Veil State **for both** (he enters it too — the time-slow becomes a contest).

**The game refuses to establish which one is the original.** Cinematography deliberately swaps shoulder and costume cues; the final blow is framed so that the survivor's identity is ambiguous; the HUD briefly shows the *other* man's health bar as the player's.

**◆ Set piece:** **confrontation with the duplicate** in the ruins during a storm, lightning revealing each to the other.

## 10 — THE VEIL
**Dhaka · Reality · Monsoon night, then underground emergency light**

**Space:** Mission 01's Dhaka, **transformed**. The same streets are subtly wrong: the green sign reads a different name, the metro runs silent, the man in white stands everywhere. Previous locations intrude as fragments (a Sundarbans channel flooding a bazaar lane; a Rangpur tea stall on a Dhaka roof). Beneath Dhaka: **the JANUS facility**.

**Reveal:** PROJECT JANUS was not a weapons programme. It was a **psychological conditioning programme** that produces operatives whose memories and identities can be rewritten. Raven is one of its products. The whole campaign may have been another controlled experiment.

**◆ Set piece:** the final underground infiltration and assault. **Lights go out. Emergency lights. Enemies attack. Full VEIL STATE.** The soundtrack disappears; only heartbeat. Rain particles (falling through a shaft), smoke, dust, debris, casings, sparks — all slowed. The game's longest interactive slow-motion chain: the player moves through a sequence of tactical decisions, never a cutscene. It collapses back into normal speed.

**Final room:** two chairs. One holds the handler. The other is empty.

> **ZAHIR:** Sit down.
> **RAVEN:** Who am I?
> **ZAHIR:** That depends on which version of you wakes up tomorrow.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

No definitive explanation. The evidence supports multiple readings — original person, manufactured identity, copy, or a real man whose memories have been rewritten many times. Carry-over choices (Heron, the colonel, the Rangpur loop, the port records) change *which evidence* is in the room, never the answer.
